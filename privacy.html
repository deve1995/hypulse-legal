Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hypulse ("Hypulse", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores and protects information when users access or use the Hypulse mobile application and related services.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Hypulse, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Social media account information that you choose to connect to Hypulse.
  • Social media profile and content data made available through authorized platform APIs.
  • Performance metrics such as views, likes, followers, engagement and content statistics where available.
  • Videos, images, captions or carousel content voluntarily submitted for AI analysis.
  • Technical information required for the operation, security and improvement of the service.

2. Social media integrations

Hypulse may allow you to connect supported social media platforms such as TikTok and Instagram.

Hypulse only accesses information permitted by the user and made available through the relevant platform's official authorization process and APIs.

Connecting a social account is optional and requires your authorization.

3. How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide social media analytics.
  • Display account and content performance.
  • Generate AI-powered content analysis.
  • Provide personalized recommendations and insights.
  • Maintain user accounts and authentication.
  • Detect abuse and protect the security of Hypulse.
  • Improve the application and its features.

4. AI content analysis

Users may voluntarily submit videos, photographs, carousel images and captions for AI-assisted analysis.

Submitted media may be temporarily processed by third-party infrastructure and artificial intelligence services solely for the purpose of generating the requested analysis.

Hypulse is designed to remove temporary analysis files after processing where technically supported.

5. Third-party services

Hypulse may rely on third-party service providers for infrastructure, authentication, data storage, artificial intelligence processing and social media integrations.

These may include services such as Supabase, Google AI services, TikTok and Meta technologies.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

6. Data storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

No electronic service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Access tokens

Where social media integrations require access tokens or refresh tokens, Hypulse intends to store sensitive authentication credentials securely on server-side infrastructure rather than exposing them directly within the client application.

8. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account history, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect the service.

Temporary media submitted for AI analysis may be deleted after processing.

9. Your choices

Users may disconnect connected social media accounts and may request deletion of their Hypulse account and associated personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

10. Third-party platforms

Hypulse is an independent application and is not owned, endorsed or operated by TikTok, Meta, Instagram or other third-party social platforms.

Use of connected platforms remains subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

11. Children's privacy

Hypulse is not intended for children below the minimum age required to use the relevant services under applicable law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Hypulse develops. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
